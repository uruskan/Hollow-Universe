# Owner playtest reports from 16 October 2026

Normalized from the owner's twelve numbered reports; numbering and requested outcomes are
preserved. Implementation suggestions such as scaling cliff meshes or using a shader remain
options to evaluate, not selected architecture. The owner intends to distribute the work
across separate chats with three subagents per chat. [Merged queue](../REPAIR_BACKLOG.md).

1. **N1 — cliff coverage and prior work.** Screenshots 1 and 2 show unacceptable cliff dressing:
   isolated repeated parts leave most of the exposed terrain face bare. Recover surviving work
   from the earlier environment chat and use the available cliff library to make coherent faces.
2. **N2 — rock identity, nodes and pickaxes.** Screenshot 3's rock cannot be harvested. Identify
   whether it is a node or decorative rock, locate the dedicated node asset package and verify
   its real use. The owner says the axe animations are now great; preserve them. Pickaxes have
   unacceptable grip, angle and apparent reach and need repair.
3. **N3 — terrain repetition.** Screenshot 4 shows easily visible repeated texture tiles.
   Fix material scale/blending/repetition and any actual terrain seams to support high fidelity.
4. **N4 — malformed cliffs.** Screenshot 5's repeating upright wall/columns are unacceptable.
   Diagnose and repair the generated shape, dressing and silhouette.
5. **N5 — cliff quality and cost.** Screenshot 6 shows expensive repeated parts that fail to
   cover the intended faces. Use efficient coherent formations, varied assets and contextual
   rocks/debris/trunks/nodes. Scaling fewer meshes or shaders are suggested possibilities.
   Measure the actual cost and preserve collision and resource rules.
6. **N6 — grass.** Grass looks sunken and poor even at Maximum, unlike the earlier reference
   images; improve its appearance and investigate cost. Screenshot 7 was referenced, but the
   attachment is identical to screenshot 6. Grass is visible in 3, 8, 9 and 10 as well.
7. **N7 — first-person tool composition.** The axe in screenshot 8 is appropriately smaller;
   other tools such as screenshot 9 occupy too much of the view. Establish professional
   viewmodel framing/projection and explain distance versus scaling rather than applying an
   arbitrary per-tool camera-distance workaround.
8. **N8 — holo and ballistic readability.** Screenshot 10's holo is too large and obstructive.
   Rework it. Preserve real bullet speed/drop and make flight/drop sufficiently visible to
   judge aim, especially incendiary ammunition, with optimized rendering rather than requiring
   every round to be visible throughout its entire life.
9. **N9 — ship attitude and gunner viewpoint.** With assist ON, a tilted Iron Wake rights
   itself when the owner leaves the helm. That is unwanted. At zero thrust preserve the chosen
   angle unattended so the owner can operate the guns. Turret seats/views feel too low to see
   targets and must be corrected.
10. **N10 — roofs.** Screenshot 11 shows unwanted roof side panels. Remove those panels and
    support joining/stacking roofs side by side, with matching collision/support behavior.
11. **N11 — gibs.** Add minimum/maximum gib settings so players can control their cost.
12. **N12 — overall performance.** Investigate low FPS and inefficient/scaling systems since
    the fork. Optimize with measured causes while retaining visual quality. Technical debt is
    a hypothesis to investigate, not an established cause from screenshots.
