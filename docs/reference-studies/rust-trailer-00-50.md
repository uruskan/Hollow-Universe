## Direct visual lessons

- The rock is a two-handed object, including the stable carry at 8.70. Its equip includes a distinct left-hand release/reseat, not a fixed closed fist welded throughout every phase. The hands support volume and roll the object for inspection.
- A hatchet is carried by its handle with one right-hand grip. Inspection can deliberately put the head broadside across the screen; that should not be mistaken for idle carry. The chop is a sequence of preparation, rapid inward travel, compact contact, recoil and a slower reposition. The upper-right windup briefly leaves the screen, but the hit and early recovery are readable.
- During chopping, the working edge, bark damage, X, chips and dust share the same small region. The visual event is not just a forearm moving while particles happen somewhere else. Wood chips differ in shape from dark stone fragments; a small luminous impact accent lasts briefly.
- Mining changes resource silhouette at a hit: the lower broken stage and moving hotspot make progress readable without a large UI overlay. The trailer largely hides gameplay HUD, so it is not evidence for removing Hollow's preserved HUD.
- Bow handling has separable equip, low carry, nock, raising/draw, hold, release and recovery. Left arm extension and bow cant leave the target line visible. Several full handling transitions are shown; simply rotating the same idle bow upright would miss most of the sequence.
- Spear thrusts project forward toward the animal. Camera pitch changes with the animal's close range, and the cast shadow grounds the player/weapon in the scene.
- Terrain is not a uniform carpet. Low grass, taller clumps, isolated flowers, ferns, deadwood, embedded stone, bare earth and shadow occupy different patches. Woodland floor undulates, so nearby ridges hide and reveal roots and rocks. Broad openings alternate with dense clumps; not every square metre gets the same density.
- Trees vary in height, lean, crown width, density and branch survival; some are bare/dead. The trunk surfaces in enlarged chopping frames have real-scale coarse bark structure and localized pale damaged bands, rather than equal-frequency stripes everywhere. Ferns and roots occupy darker near-ground pockets.
- Landscape has foreground/midground/background separation: crisp shaded nearby objects, sunlit openings, then paler distant hills/tree masses. Dry ochre grass, green ferns and broadleaf trees, gray stone and wet dark shore are distinct material groups.
- Waterline transitions contain tiny reflective ripples/wet sand, pebbles, exposed stones and dry reeds. The shore is a broad changing material region rather than one uniform bright water plane meeting a flat grass edge.
- Primitive building communicates construction via poles, braces, lashings, board grain and log ends. A short burst signals completion. Blue/ red placement states and distinct preview shape are immediately legible; small attachments use small previews.
- A base is shown as a coherent sequence: foundation, enclosure, upgrade, door, lock, storage/claim object, box, sleeping bag. The montage demonstrates relationships but does not prove exact costs, crafting mechanics, persistence or networking.
- Animals occupy habitats and change the player's handling decisions: deer on a skyline requires aim, wolf's close approach demands directional tracking, bear's charge outruns a leisurely bow nock. Specific AI rules cannot be recovered from these edited shots.
- Landmarks work through silhouette and route composition: road lines, overhead linear structures, towers and a distant dome. The environment itself provides wayfinding without an always-present map overlay.

## Proposed application, not claims of existing Hollow behavior

1. Keep the user-accepted metal axe/pick poses as reference baselines. Review other models by their actual silhouettes, handle dimensions and hand contacts; do not apply one transform to every item.
2. Establish a contact-frame acceptance capture that overlays the impact socket and confirmed target location only in diagnostics. Evaluate the axe edge on bark and pick tip on stone at close, normal and maximum reach. Preserve server reach/timing.
3. Build or select complete hand performances: rock support/reseat, axe windup/contact/recovery, spear thrust, bow nock/draw/release. Use modest whole-rig locomotion offsets so arms and held object stay together. A motionless source-bound carry will not convey the reference's walking/running handling.
4. Capture real-input idle, walk, brisk run, stop, turn, equip, swing hit/miss and recovery loops; sample every frame around contact. Distinguish the montage's camera/edits from ordinary play.
5. Treat terrain/lighting lessons as a separate measured environment pass: density patches, local relief, coarse-vs-fine material scale, warm/cool light hierarchy and distance haze. Do not rewrite unrelated terrain generation from trailer interpretation alone.
6. Keep the ground UI as required by DESIGN.md. Trailer marketing captions, branding, hidden HUD and cinematic cuts are not UI requirements.
7. Review audio independently from actual sound playback. These silent frame sheets cannot establish impact timbre, whoosh shape, ambience, dynamic range, sync or volume.
