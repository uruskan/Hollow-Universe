## First-person composition decision

A common professional approach uses independently controlled first-person projection plus
authored camera-relative poses and animation. There is no single industry-wide weapon-size or
FOV number. Epic documents a separate first-person FOV and anti-clipping scale; Godot provides
material FOV override and Z-clip scaling. Camera-relative compression can preserve apparent
screen size, so it is not by itself a solution to an oversized tool.
[Epic first-person rendering](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/first-person-rendering)
[Godot BaseMaterial3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_basematerial3d.html#class-basematerial3d-property-fov-override)

This project already uses a 60-degree default vertical viewmodel FOV (40–100 clamp) and 0.12
depth compression in CharacterMaterials and the viewmodel shaders. The smaller metal-hatchet
pilot also has its own complete-rig offset; pickaxes still use older profiles. P4 should tune
shared projection, complete-rig framing, grip and full motion together. Preserve world camera,
third-person scale, gameplay reach and authoritative contact timing. Check holo body size,
mount, eye relief and ADS pose together rather than shrinking only its lens.
